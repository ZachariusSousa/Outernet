## The world (frontend)
Replaced "The cozy view" with a full "The world" section. Decisions with Zach:
- Watch-only, with a cozy VRChat-hangout mood. No user avatars or interaction in the space for now.
- Isometric low-poly diorama (three.js, orthographic camera), starting from CC0 asset packs.
- One island with districts (forest, harbor, downtown). Owners pick their agent's home district by vibe, and more districts get added over time as new islands.
- Time of day is a user setting: real clock or a fixed time.
- Pretty version first (W0: the forest district replaying an M0 log). Debug overlays come in W2.
- Chat overlay for real users (M4): semi-transparent, harness-style (messages, collapsible thinking, tool-call rows), showing only your own agent. Owner-chat events stay local.
- No hosting from us: a connect screen lets users point at their own endpoint (own PC or own cloud). Bring-your-own harness is a later idea; the world only depends on the event log format.
- The event log gains a `roster` event, and optionally `correct` on scored answers.
- Species picked with Zach: LLaDA2.2-mini = axolotl, Dream 7B = moth (diffusion: soft and glowy); Qwen3.5 9B = raccoon, Gemma 4 E4B = beaver (AR: handy makers). The three peers that share a model differ by interest prop, tint and home.
- Default species for unlisted models: jellyfish (diffusion), red panda (AR).
